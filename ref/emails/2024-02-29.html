Hello DPW Friends,

The organizing committees for the upcoming DPW-8/AePW-4 have continued preparations since the kickoff meeting at SciTech. For those of you that attended, thank you!

As the scope of the workshop has matured, it has become clear to the organizing committees that the workshop would benefit from a delay of one year and be held at AVIATION 2026 in San Diego, CA. The intention of the schedule change is to provide additional time to address the challenges of each working group. The expectation is that working group meetings will begin within the next few months.

If you are interested in participating, we invite you to fill out this survey to indicate your interest in which working group(s) you are most interested. This survey will be used to populate the working group email distribution lists. While one can always be added to a distribution list later, it is easiest to get as accurate of a list as possible now.
